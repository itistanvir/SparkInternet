Gaming

Ping you can
build a strategy on.

A good game is decided in a few hundred milliseconds. We route game traffic over the shortest path to BDIX and regional servers, keep cache nodes for the big titles inside our own network, and hold the jitter flat so the number you see in the corner stays where it was in round one.

  • Stable ping, not just a low one

    Latency is held within a narrow band through peak evening hours, so aim and movement stay predictable.

  • Cache nodes inside the network

    Valorant, PUBG, Free Fire and Steam updates pull from local cache instead of crossing the international link.

  • Upload that keeps up

    Stream your match to Facebook or YouTube while you play, without the frame rate collapsing.

  • Link restored fast

    Fibre faults are located and patched by a field team based in Khulna, not dispatched from Dhaka.

Everyday browsing

Pages open before
you finish typing.

Most of the internet is small requests, not big files, so we tune for the first byte. Local DNS, BDIX peering and clean fibre mean a news site, a class on Zoom, a Facebook video and four phones on Wi-Fi all feel instant at the same time.

  • No evening slowdown

    Capacity is planned for peak hours, so 9pm looks the same on a speed test as 9am.

  • 4K video without buffering

    YouTube, Netflix and Prime Video start at full resolution and stay there.

  • Calls that hold

    Zoom, Teams and WhatsApp video stay connected through a long meeting or a class.

  • Every device, at once

    Phones, TV, CCTV and a laptop on the same line without anyone having to wait their turn.
